## What this submission demonstrates

- Spark Bronze/Silver/Gold pipeline over live third-party job data.
- Quality scoring, deduplication, skill extraction, and curated Gold serving.
- Unstructured job descriptions embedded with MiniLM and stored in Lakebase pgvector.
- A user-facing Databricks App with semantic job matching and application tracking.
- An MCP-powered AI agent that retrieves jobs and performs real persistent actions in Lakebase.
